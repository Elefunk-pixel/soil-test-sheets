          About this section
          The sample moisture is not part of the curve. With an estimated OMC, Fill added % spreads the points evenly either side of it and works out how much water each one needs. You can still change any added % by hand.
          w = (tin + wet − tin + dry) ÷ (tin + dry − tin) × 100
          added % for a point = its target w − sample w

          Test details

          Compaction points

          One column per point
          How this table is worked out
          water to add = dry soil × added % ÷ 100
          dry soil = batch ÷ (1 + sample w ÷ 100)
          target w = sample w + added %
          wet density = (mould + wet soil − mould) ÷ volume
          dry density = wet density ÷ (1 + w ÷ 100)
          Moisture is the mean of that point's tins in the moisture table below.

          Moisture tins

          Oven-dried, one column per tin
          About this table
          w = (tin + wet − tin + dry) ÷ (tin + dry − tin) × 100
          Add a row for a second tin on the same point. The point moisture is the mean of its tins.
